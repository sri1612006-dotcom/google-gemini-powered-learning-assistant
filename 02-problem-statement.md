# 2. Problem Statement

Students often spend significant time searching multiple sources for explanations, summaries and practice questions. Information may be difficult to understand, too detailed, or not matched to the learner's immediate requirement.

Traditional learning applications may provide fixed content, but they cannot always respond dynamically to a student's question. EduGenie addresses this problem by providing an interactive AI assistant capable of generating contextual educational responses.

## Problem
- Difficulty finding quick and understandable explanations.
- Repetitive searching across multiple resources.
- Limited personalized practice.
- Fixed learning content in conventional systems.
- Need for an interactive revision assistant.

## Proposed Solution
EduGenie combines a web interface with Google's Gemini generative AI service to provide dynamic educational assistance.

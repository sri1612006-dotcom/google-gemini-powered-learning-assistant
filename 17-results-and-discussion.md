# 17. Results and Discussion

The completed prototype demonstrates how generative AI can be integrated into an educational web application.

## Observed Capabilities
- Users can submit learning questions.
- The application can generate explanations.
- The same architecture can support summaries and quizzes.
- The modular design allows additional learning features.

## Discussion
The quality of generated output depends on the prompt, model behavior, available context and the accuracy of the underlying AI system. Therefore, the application is intended as a learning aid rather than a replacement for teachers, textbooks or verified academic resources.
